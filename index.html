FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before running hwid-reset on your machine.

Is hwid-reset free and open source?+

Yes. hwid-reset is completely free and the source is publicly available. There are no license keys, no subscription tiers and no telemetry — you can inspect every change the tool makes to your system.

Which Windows versions are supported?+

hwid-reset supports Windows 10 and Windows 11 on both x64 and ARM64 builds. Administrator privileges are required, and Secure Boot must be disabled for SMBIOS and disk serial spoofing to take effect.

Do profiles really survive a reboot?+

Yes. Profiles are stored with a boot-time reapply service, so the spoofed disk serials, SMBIOS values, MAC addresses and registry entries are restored automatically after every restart — including driver reloads and Windows updates.

Can I undo the changes and restore my original hardware IDs?+

Absolutely. A rollback snapshot is created before any modification. From the profiles screen you can restore the original hardware identity at any time, or keep multiple profiles and switch between them.
